XXIIIAwakening

Beauty

A burden to carry for her appearance is intoxicating.

She brings and enjoys lots of pleasure and how intoxicating the pleasure.

But the more beautiful, the more toxic.

Where toxicity thrives, beauty fades and where beauty fades, pleasure stops.

All that is left is bland and boring.

Suddenly she is no more beatiful even though she once was pleasurable.

The beauty is gone, the burden remains.

Yet, just like beauty, the boring can be intoxicating.

She is indifferent.

She remains beautiful.

She is delusional.
