IVReflection

The-woman

Mothers are perceived to be angels, death preserves that perception but women do not feel the same after that fact.

It is soul crushing to know that having a mutual connection with a person isn’t enough because regardless of emotions, they could choose someone else.

Never hesitate to dispense of a woman if the need arises because she is a ruthless opportunistic creature that cares about nothing but herself. If you find yourself dependent in any situation, romantic or business, that is your cue to leave.
